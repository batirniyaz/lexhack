## Conclusions

1. **Art. 27¹ is confirmed verbatim (ZRU-1125 of 26.03.2026, in force 27.03.2026).** Mandatory storage in Uzbekistan
   covers only three categories: biometric data, genetic data, and data of users of telecom operators working in
   Uzbekistan. All other personal data may be stored and processed abroad if **one** of three conditions is met:
   (a) an adequate country on the Cabinet list, (b) standard contractual clauses or binding corporate rules meeting
   requirements of the authorized body, (c) international standards from a list approved by the authorized body.
2. **CMR No. 415 of 29.07.2026 is read on lex.uz (published 03.08.2026, No. 09/26/415/0807).** The list has **49
   entries: 30 EU/EEA states, 15 other states and 4 territories (Guernsey, Jersey, Isle of Man, Faroe Islands), and
   the USA with an asterisk — only for companies in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.** Hong Kong is on the list;
   mainland China, Kazakhstan, UAE, Turkey, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan, Australia, India are **not**. The official text
   exists only in Uzbek (lex.uz says so on the Russian page).
3. **Corrections to the passport.** (a) The US is *on* the list with a condition — passport V-12 wording is right in
   substance, but the draft file listed 31 countries; the full list is in `rules/countries.yaml`. (b) The 24 h / 72 h
   notification in CMR 415 applies to **leaks during cross-border transfer**, not to all breaches. (c) CMR 415 names
   the **Migration and Personalization Department under the Ministry of Internal Affairs** as the authorized state
   body, while art. 8 of the law (current edition on lex.uz) still names the **State Personalization Center under
   the Cabinet of Ministers**. The other acts show the function has moved to the MoI: CMR No. 821 of 24.12.2025
   rewrote the State Register regulation with «vakolatli organ — O‘zbekiston Respublikasi Ichki ishlar vazirligi
   huzuridagi Migratsiya va personallashtirish departamenti», and PP-14 of 19.01.2026 replaced "Personalization
   Agency under the Ministry of Justice" with this Department across decrees and plans to give it the power to draw
   up administrative protocols for violations of ZRU-547 (draft amendment to the Administrative Code, due May
   2026). → **De facto authority: the MoI Department; art. 8 of the law is simply not updated** (V-03).
4. **Two 2026 amendments the passport does not mention yet:** (a) ZRU-1158 of 13.07.2026 — the Constitutional Law
   **"On the Tashkent International Financial Centre"** (in force 25.07.2026) — added art. 3 part 3 to ZRU-547:
   **on a territory with a special legal regime set by a Constitutional Law, personal data is governed by
   different rules.** Today that means the Tashkent International Financial Centre; its own rules are to be
   adopted as "decisions of the Financial Centre" within 12 months (art. 31 of ZRU-1158), none found yet.
   (b) ZRU-1174 of 11.09.2026 changed art. 25 para 14 (special data of orphans and children without parental care,
   guardianship) — no effect on website checks. → Probo must not apply ZRU-547 blindly to TIFC participants.
5. **V-19 closed:** Google LLC, Microsoft, Amazon.com, Cloudflare — DPF **Active**; Meta Platforms — **Active,
   re-certification under review** (checked on dataprivacyframework.gov, 21.11.2026). So US trackers of these five
   companies fall under the asterisk condition of CMR 415.
6. **Not yet adopted (checked in the local corpus up to 10.11 and in the lex.uz RSS feed 21.10–19.11.2026):** the
   requirements for standard contractual clauses and binding corporate rules (CMR 415 item 7, due within three
   months, i.e. by about 29.10.2026 — **overdue**) and the list of international standards (art. 27¹ part 3). Until they exist, conditions (b) and (c) cannot be proven by any operator — but
   art. 15 still allows transfer to non-adequate countries with the subject's consent. → PD-LOC findings for
   non-listed countries stay 🟡 ("the legal basis must be confirmed"), not 🔴.

### 8. V-16 — whose country counts: the server's or the company's? (interpretation)

- The law defines cross-border transfer by **territory**: "за пределы территории Республики Узбекистан" / "на
  территорию иностранных государств" (art. 15); CMR 415 lists **states and territories**. → The primary test is
  **where the data physically goes** (the recipient's territory).
- The only company-based test is the asterisk for the **USA**: there, the recipient **company** must be in the DPF.
- **Telegram bot as the recipient of leads:** Telegram's operating company is in the UAE (not on the list), and
  where a given bot's messages are stored is not visible from outside. Under the territory test the answer depends
  on the data centre, which we cannot see → Probo should report **both** the server country (by IP) and the
  operator's country, and set the finding to 🟡 "undetermined — the legal basis must be confirmed". This matches
  how WhoGetsMyData separates "jurisdiction" from "server country" (`competitors-global-features.md`).
- Status: **interpretation by research, NOT VERIFIED by a lawyer.** Role 2 decides.

### 9. V-18 — are visitors of a telecom operator's website "users of its services"? (interpretation)

- The official Uzbek wording is «телекоммуникациялар операторларидаги уларнинг хизматларидан фойдаланувчи
  жисмоний шахсларнинг маълумотлари» — literally "data of individuals who use their services, **held at** telecom
  operators". It targets data that the operator holds about its subscribers/users.
- Reading 1 (narrow): an anonymous visitor of the public website is not yet a "user of telecom services";
  trackers on public pages → 🟡 like any site.
- Reading 2 (wide): visitors who are subscribers (self-care cabinet, top-up, tariff pages with login) are users;
  their data sent abroad by trackers → 🔴 (mandatory localization).
- The Law "On Telecommunications" ZRU-1015 of 27.12.2024, art. 3, defines the user (official Uzbek / unofficial
  Russian):
  > «telekommunikatsiyalar xizmatlaridan foydalanuvchi (bundan buyon matnda foydalanuvchi deb yuritiladi) —
  > telekommunikatsiyalar xizmatlarining iste’molchisi bo‘lgan yuridik yoki jismoniy shaxs»
  > «пользователь услуг телекоммуникаций (далее — пользователь) — юридическое или физическое лицо, являющееся
  > потребителем услуг телекоммуникаций»
- So a "user" is a **consumer of telecom services** (a subscriber), not anyone who opens the operator's website.
  This supports Reading 1 for anonymous visitors and Reading 2 for logged-in subscribers. Still a lawyer's call.
  Status: **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** (definition confirmed; its application to website visitors is interpretation).
  Product suggestion: for sites in the telecom sector, raise 🔴 only for pages behind login (personal cabinet,
  top-up with a subscriber number), 🟡 for public pages, and show the reason.

## Questions for the team / contradictions with the passport

- **Q-L1 (Role 2, V-03).** Art. 8 still names the State Personalization Center; CMR 821, PP-14 and CMR 415 name
  the MoI Migration and Personalization Department. Research reading: the Department is the authority in practice.
  Confirm the wording we use in reports and in R-08.
- **Q-L2 (Role 2).** CMR 415 item 4 para 2 allows transfer to non-listed states "in any form" if conditions of the
  authorized body are met; art. 15 allows it with the subject's consent. How do these interact for a website (is a
  consent banner with a cross-border clause enough)? This decides PD-LOC-02's level and fix text.
- **Q-L3 (Role 2).** SCC/BCR requirements (item 7) and the list of international standards (art. 27¹ part 3) were
  not found up to 19.11.2026 (the item 7 deadline has passed). The "how to fix" text should say these routes are
  not available yet and point to an adequate-country provider or the subject's consent (art. 15).
- **Q-L4 (Role 1).** `rules/countries.yaml` (this commit) replaces the draft: the code will now treat all 48 states
  as adequate and the US as conditional. Because the list is complete, `not_in_known_list` can be read as "not
  listed" — the YAML carries `complete: true` for that; the code does not read this key yet.
- **Q-L5 (closed).** ZRU-1174 (11.09.2026) changed only art. 25 para 14 (orphans, guardianship) — no effect on
  PD-PII-01.
